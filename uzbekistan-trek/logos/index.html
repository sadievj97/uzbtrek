Logo options

Five directions for Uzbekistan Trek, each shown on white, navy and yellow, at small sizes and as an Instagram avatar. All text is converted to outlines, so the SVG files work anywhere without fonts.

A. Khatam star Recommended

The eight-point star from Uzbek girih tilework, the pattern you see on every madrasa wall. Simple enough to read at favicon size, clearly Central Asian, no copied landmark.

Recommended as the main logo. Works on the website header, on Instagram as the avatar, on merch and on stamps for the group photos.

Used on the main yellow-and-navy site.

Files in the repo: logo-a-star-mark.svg, logo-a-star-lockup.svg, logo-a-star-lockup-white.svg

B. Registan skyline

Three madrasa portals, a dome and four minarets: the Registan drawn as one line. Instantly says Samarkand, which is also the founder's hometown.

Strong as a wordmark lockup and on printed pieces. Too detailed to work as a tiny favicon, so pair it with the star or the monogram for small sizes.

Used on the Registan gold and blue style.

Files in the repo: logo-b-registan-mark.svg, logo-b-registan-lockup.svg, logo-b-registan-lockup-h.svg

C. Travel stamp

A round passport stamp: UZBEKISTAN TREK across the top, SINCE 2025 below, a madrasa portal in the middle. Feels like a souvenir from the trip.

Great as a badge on photos, stickers, certificates and the Instagram avatar. Text gets small below about 48 pixels, so it is a second mark, not the header logo.

Used on the parallax page.

Files in the repo: logo-c-stamp-navy.svg, logo-c-stamp-gold-on-navy.svg

D. UT monogram tile

The initials UT on a glazed tile with a thin inset border. Reads well at every size and looks at home next to university brands without copying any of them.

Best for favicons, app icons and avatars. Shown in crimson here; it works in navy or turquoise too.

Used on the Harvard crimson and MIT style.

Files in the repo: logo-d-monogram-crimson.svg, logo-d-monogram-lockup.svg

E. Ikat wordmark

The name in heavy type over a band of ikat diamonds in red, saffron, turquoise and navy, the colors of Uzbek silk.

Good for banners, slide decks and the footer. There is no separate symbol, so it needs one of the other marks for the avatar.

Not used in a preview yet.

Files in the repo: logo-e-ikat-wordmark.svg

How to choose

  • Pick one main logo for the header and one symbol for small sizes. A and D work alone at every size; B, C and E need a partner symbol for the browser tab.
  • Every style preview can take any of these logos; swapping it is one line in each style's build script.
  • None of them uses university marks, shields or seals, so the brand stays clearly independent.
