What your group gets

  • A private itinerary on your dates
  • Hotels, meals, fast trains, transfers and licensed guides
  • Company, university and cultural visits matched to your field (subject to approval)
  • An Uzbek graduate student host with your group all week (subject to confirmation and availability)

  • An organizer kit: info-session slides, a flyer and a registration page

Who does what

You

Big group questions

What if fewer than 10 people sign up?

We can still run your trip as a private small-group trip, or your travelers can join one of our planned departures.

Can we travel outside spring break?

Yes. Most groups travel during a school break, but we can run your trek in any month.

Can our group meet government officials?

The standard program focuses on companies, universities and/or culture (subject to approval). Formal government meetings are not part of it.

Can we change the route?

Yes. Add days, add Khiva or the Fergana Valley, or spend a night at a desert yurt camp. Your quote changes accordingly.

Who collects the money?

Each participant pays the operator directly through our registration page. Organizers never handle payments.

Is the trip affiliated with our university?

No. Your group organizes itself and travels independently. Please do not present the trip as an official university program.
